A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poster-for-a-place-that-i-2-20261121-0539) bounty entry.
`main.py` draws a vintage travel poster for **LUMINARA** — a cliff city above a
glowing lagoon where the sea keeps the light long after sunset — entirely with
PIL: gradient dusk sky, a full moon, the Crystal Spire lighthouse, sailboats
in the bay, and hand-set type. No image is copied in; every pixel is generated
by the program.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py            # writes /output/entry.png
python3 main.py entry.png  # or write to a path of your choice
```

Output: one PNG, 768 × 1024, well under the 1 MiB gate.

## The place

**LUMINARA** — *Where the sea keeps the light.* Beyond the edge of every map,
a city of glass spires stands on black cliffs above a lagoon that glows after
dark. Its landmark, the Crystal Spire, sweeps a beam of warm light across the
bay each evening, and the sailboats come home by it.
